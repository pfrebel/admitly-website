UK
Destination 01 · United Kingdom

Global prestige, built for speed

Home to some of the most recognized university brands on Earth, and degree structures designed to get you into the workforce faster.

World-leading institutionsOxford, Cambridge, Imperial, LSE, and UCL routinely top global rankings and open doors everywhere.
Shorter, focused degrees3-year bachelor's and 1-year master's mean less time to get into the workforce than most alternatives.
Post-study work visaThe Graduate Route currently offers up to 2 years of UK work rights after graduating (18 months from 2027): real runway to launch a career.
London as a career engineUnmatched access to finance, consulting, law, and startups in one of the world's top business capitals.
CH
Destination 02 · Switzerland

Elite outcomes, exceptional living

Small in size, outsized in impact: Swiss universities pair rigorous technical education with some of the best quality of life anywhere in the world.

World-class STEM & businessETH Zurich and other Swiss institutions rank among the best globally for engineering, computer science, and finance.
Surprisingly affordable tuitionPublic university tuition is a fraction of UK or US costs, even for international students.
Safety and quality of lifeSwitzerland ranks in the global top 5 for quality of life, with Zurich cited as one of the safest, most student-friendly cities in Europe.
Central, multilingual, high-earningClose to Zurich's finance sector and Basel's pharma industry, with strong graduate salaries across STEM fields.
EU
Destination 03 · Mainland Europe

Affordable, diverse, wide open

From near-free tuition in Germany to specialist programs in France, Italy, and the Netherlands, mainland Europe offers some of the best value in global higher education.

Low or no tuitionGermany offers 1,900+ English-taught master's programs, many with little more than a small semester fee.
Thousands of English-taught programsThe Netherlands alone offers 2,100+ programs taught fully in English, from business to liberal arts.
Specialist strengthsSciences Po for politics, Bocconi for finance, KU Leuven for research: standout programs across the continent.
Lower cost of living, easy travelGenerally more affordable than London or Zurich, with the whole continent a train ride away.
