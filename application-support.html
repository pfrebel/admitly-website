Standard vs. Premium

Every Application Support client gets expert help with essays, strategy, and the practical steps of moving abroad. Programs that admit around individual research fit and supervisor availability — Oxford and Cambridge above all — reward a different, deeper kind of preparation.

Premium is required for Oxbridge applications and recommended for other highly competitive, research-intensive programs.

Standard

Comprehensive support for the vast majority of undergraduate and taught-master's applications.

  • Personal statement coaching One-on-one guidance to craft a compelling, authentic personal statement that stands out to admissions committees.
  • Essay coaching Feedback and editing on any supplementary essays your target programs require.
  • Application strategy From UCAS to individual university portals, we manage timelines, requirements, and documentation so nothing slips through the cracks.
  • Standardized test strategy A study and timing plan to hit the scores your target programs require — TOEFL, GRE, GMAT.
  • Visa & relocation guidance Support navigating student visas, accommodation, and the practical steps of moving abroad for university.
⚡ We reply within one working day.

Premium

Required for Oxbridge

Built for maximizing your chances of admission to the most competitive programs — where the department, the supervisor, and the fit matter as much as the grades.

  • Supervisor matching We know the department and which faculty has capacity to take on master's students. Admission is subject to supervisor availability, so we find the right fit early.
  • Research narrative alignment We match your research interests to specific research units and realistic thesis supervisors.
  • Transcript audit A deep dive on your coursework (e.g. multivariable calculus, linear algebra, econometrics) to make the case for readiness for the first-year coursework at Oxford and similarly rigorous programs.
  • Standardized test strategy A study and timing plan to hit the scores your target programs require — TOEFL, GRE, GMAT.
  • Personal statement — deep-dive Built on your supervisor match, research interests, and prior coursework. This is where you can gain the most at this stage of the application — we help make it convincing, credible, and effective at conveying your skills.
  • Writing sample audit A full structural edit of the mandatory writing sample (e.g. a 2,000-word paper) — making sure the mathematical notation, methodology, and citations are all correct and up to standard.
  • Referee strategy Custom briefing notes for each referee, telling them exactly what to emphasize — quantitative ranking, research potential, fit for the program.
  • Reference diversification An optimal strategy for using varying referees across different programs, to hedge against any one reference letter falling short.
  • College choice College selection based on departmental links, available funding, and accommodation — factoring in your personal preferences and priorities as well.
  • Scholarship search & applications We help you look for scholarships and, where applicable, help you with the application for them too.
  • Milestone tracking A structured plan through the submission deadline, then continued support after — college placement, conditional offers, and funding acceptance.
⚡ We reply within 4 hours during UK working hours, and within 12 hours otherwise.
